1. Stop the press and confirm the previous lot is cleared
2. Cool the old mold and disconnect water and hot runner lines
3. Lift out the old mold and clean the platen faces
4. Mount the new mold and torque the clamps to specification
5. Reconnect water and heat the hot runner to setpoint
6. Run a dry cycle at low clamp pressure
7. Mold the first article and measure the critical dimensions
8. Release the lot and record the changeover time
