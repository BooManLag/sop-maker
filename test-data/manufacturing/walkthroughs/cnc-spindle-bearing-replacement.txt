1. Apply lockout and tagout to the machine and bleed the drawbar air
2. Remove the spindle cartridge with the crane and mount it on the bearing stand
3. Press off the worn bearings and clean the shaft journals
4. Fit the new angular contact bearing set and set the preload to specification
5. Reinstall the cartridge and reconnect coolant and lubrication lines
6. Measure spindle runout at the taper and confirm it is within 5 microns
7. Record runout and close the work order
