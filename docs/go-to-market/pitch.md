The Exception Scan, from Successful Exceptions (working name), new software from an established company for field service organisations.
In 6 weeks, from a one-time export of 12 to 24 months of job records your team pulls itself (no IT integration, no cameras), we find where technicians on one equipment line repeatedly depart from your SOP, and whether those jobs get fewer 30-day callbacks. Each comparison is made within the same technician (their off-SOP jobs vs their by-the-book jobs), the same equipment model and the same site type, then re-checked on a held-out period, so "senior techs are just better" is ruled out rather than assumed.
Week 1 is a data-readiness check: if your records can't support the analysis, we stop and you pay nothing.
You get up to 5 findings, each with sample size, effect size and confidence interval; the full method and analysis notebook for your own analyst to audit; and a document-control-ready change request for any finding you choose to trial. Findings are anonymised at technician level, written commitment that nothing is used for discipline, and nothing changes your SOP without your approval.
Price: $12,000. If no finding survives the held-out check, you get the $12,000 back. If you continue, the fee is credited in full to a $30,000/year region subscription (read-only connector, monthly refresh, quarterly findings review with our analyst, validation tracker).
