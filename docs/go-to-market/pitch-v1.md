Successful Exceptions (working name), new software for field service organisations, from an established company.
It reads 12 to 24 months of the job records you already have (parts used, time on job, checklists, technician notes) and outcomes (first-time fix, callbacks within 30 days) from Salesforce Field Service, ServiceNow or IFS. No new hardware, no cameras.
It finds the places where your technicians repeatedly do the job differently from your SOP, and checks whether those jobs stick better than by-the-book ones, adjusted for technician, asset and site.
You get a short list of "successful exceptions" to investigate, and an optional one-question follow-up to the technicians involved asking what they did and why. Nothing changes your SOP without your approval.
Price: $36,000 a year per service region or equipment line, with the first 12 weeks of setup and the first findings review included.
