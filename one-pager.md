# Good Exception (working name): one page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Year 1 operating loss of $723,400; 3 of 100 simulated buyers buy (3%, the bar is 25%). The ask below is for a capped test, not the full build.

**The problem.** Field service organisations document how a job *should* be done. Their best technicians quietly do it differently, and some of those differences get fewer callbacks. Nobody measures which. When senior techs retire, the better way walks out with them. Compliance tools ask "who broke the SOP?" Nobody asks "who broke it *and was right*?"

**The customer.** Heads of service at commercial building-systems companies (HVAC, elevators, fire/security) and directors of service operations at industrial equipment makers' service organisations, running Salesforce Field Service, ServiceNow or IFS. They review first-time fix and callbacks every week.

**The offer and price.**
- **The Exception Scan: $12,000, 6 weeks, one equipment line.** It runs from an export the customer pulls (no IT integration). Each technician is compared against themselves, then re-checked on a held-out period. The customer gets up to 5 findings with n, effect and interval, plus the analysis notebook and document-control change requests.
- **Risk reversal:** pay nothing if week-1 readiness fails; full refund if no finding survives the held-out check (criteria in writing).
- **Then $30,000/yr per region** (Scan fee credited). Design-partner price for the first 5: $6,000, in return for a reference. Closes 31 March 2027.

The levers, from the CFO's what-ifs: a 3-person team → break-even 34; a $36K/yr price → break-even 33; both → break-even 26, +25% margin at plan.

**The evidence.**
- **Board:** 3× FUND IF, average 4.3/10. Condition #1: back-test on real records.
- **Panel:** 100 simulated buyers. Trust (70%), not price, is the blocker. The Scan offer moved the simulated buy rate from 3% to 20%.
- **Competitor gap:** nobody in field service ranks *practices against the SOP* by outcome. Aquant ranks people, DeepHow does drift analysis but only for manufacturing, and the FSM platforms hold the data but sell generic AI.
- **Real test:** none yet. That's the ask.

**What the money is for.** Not a build yet. Five months of the existing team, about $20,000 to deliver 5 design-partner Scans at cost, and the parent's legal templates. The $180K connector build and $40K security attestation wait for the result.

**The ask.** Approve a 5-month, capped design-partner test from 16 November 2026, with these kill/continue lines:
- 3 paid Scans by 31 Jan 2027.
- 2 of the first 3 pass readiness by 28 Feb.
- 1 finding validated by a customer's head of service by 31 Mar.
- 1 subscription or paid trial by 30 Apr.

Pass the 28 Feb and 31 Mar lines → fund the build. Miss them → stop.
